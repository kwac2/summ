Privacy Policy

  1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CWAA Strategic Intelligence Group ("CWAA", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data in connection with our expert services in England. We are committed to processing personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

By using our services, visiting our website, or otherwise interacting with us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

  1. Data Controller

CWAA Strategic Intelligence Group is the data controller for the personal data we process. This means we determine the purposes and means of processing your personal data.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data protection practices, you may contact us using the details provided in the "Contact Us" section below.

  1. Personal Data We May Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of personal data, depending on your relationship with us and how you interact with us:

3.1 Identification and Contact Information

  • Name, title, and position
  • Business or professional address
  • Email address and telephone numbers

3.2 Professional and Business Information

  • Employer or organisation details
  • Job role, expertise, and professional background
  • Information contained in your CV or professional profile (where provided)

3.3 Service-Related Information

  • Details about your enquiry and engagement with our expert services
  • Communications and correspondence with us (including emails, meeting notes, and messages)
  • Information we receive in the course of providing strategic intelligence, advisory, or expert services

3.4 Technical and Usage Data

  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Browser type, version, and settings
  • Date, time, and duration of your visits to our website
  • Pages viewed and navigation paths

3.5 Sensitive and Special Category Data In principle, we do not seek to collect special categories of personal data (such as data relating to health, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, or criminal records). Where such information is relevant and lawfully required in the context of expert or investigative services, we will only process it when permitted by law and, where necessary, with your explicit consent or under another applicable legal basis.

  1. How We Collect Personal Data

We may obtain personal data in the following ways:

  • Directly from you, when you contact us, request our services, attend our meetings or events, or otherwise communicate with us
  • From your organisation, colleagues, or other professional contacts in the context of our expert services
  • From public sources, such as professional networking sites, corporate websites, public registers, and publications
  • From third parties, such as partners, consultants, service providers, and other professional advisers, where lawful to do so
  • Automatically, through cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website (subject to your cookie preferences)
  1. Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data only when we have a valid legal basis under the UK GDPR. These include:

  • Performance of a contract: where processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests or those of a third party, and those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to comply with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Consent: where you have given clear and specific consent for a particular purpose (you may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal)
  1. Purposes for Which We Use Personal Data

We may use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, manage, and improve our expert and strategic intelligence services
  • To evaluate and respond to requests for proposals, tenders, and service enquiries
  • To manage our relationship with clients, prospects, and professional contacts
  • To conduct research, analysis, and due diligence relevant to our expert services
  • To communicate with you about our services, events, and updates, where lawful
  • To administer and protect our business, including troubleshooting, data analysis, testing, and system maintenance
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and professional obligations
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims
  1. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Data

We may share your personal data with:

  • Members, employees, and consultants of CWAA who require access to the data to perform their roles
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, accountants, or other experts, where necessary for the provision of our services or for legal or compliance reasons
  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf (e.g., IT hosting, email, document management, analytics, or administrative services), subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards
  • Business partners or counterparties involved in a specific project, engagement, or transaction, where necessary and lawful
  • Regulatory bodies, law enforcement agencies, courts, or other authorities, where required by law or where necessary to protect our rights, your rights, or the rights of others

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

  1. International Transfers

Our operations and service providers may be located outside the United Kingdom. Where personal data is transferred to countries that do not provide an equivalent level of data protection to that of the UK, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Standard contractual clauses approved by the UK authorities
  • Transfers to organisations that have implemented recognised data protection frameworks

You may contact us for more information about international transfers and the safeguards in place.

  1. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • The duration of our engagement and any subsequent period required for record-keeping
  • The period necessary to comply with legal, regulatory, or professional obligations
  • The limitation periods for potential legal claims

When personal data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it, unless we are required to retain it by law.

  1. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include:

  • Access controls and authentication procedures
  • Encryption and secure storage
  • Regular review of security practices and systems
  • Staff training and confidentiality obligations

However, no transmission of information over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data transmitted to or from our systems.

  1. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have certain rights in relation to your personal data, subject to applicable restrictions and exemptions. These may include:

  • Right of access: to obtain confirmation as to whether we process your personal data and to receive a copy of it
  • Right to rectification: to have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected
  • Right to erasure: to request deletion of your personal data, where there is no lawful reason for us to continue processing it
  • Right to restriction: to request that we limit the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability: to receive certain personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to transmit it to another controller, where technically feasible
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on our legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below. We may need to verify your identity before responding to your request.

  1. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Enable essential site functionality
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve our content and services

Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or any tools provided on our website. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

  1. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps to delete such information.

  1. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on our website with an updated "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we process personal data.

  1. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our handling of personal data, please contact CWAA Strategic Intelligence Group using the contact details published on our official website.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data. For more information, please visit www.ico.org.uk.
